Connecting to the live event list…

Where the data comes from

A daily web check of organiser websites, venue calendars and event listings. Each event links to the page its dates came from and says whether the dates are confirmed, unconfirmed or estimated.

How courses are matched

Events are rated against the modules in ITE's official course outlines for College East's SEIT Higher Nitec and Work-Study Diploma courses, as a strong, good or related fit; each rating names the modules it links to. Pick a course in the Course box to see only the events that suit it, best fit first. Each event's details list every course that should attend.

Before you register

Always confirm dates, eligibility and fees on the organiser's site. Registration links go straight to the organiser.
